Railway & SSC Mensuration Identities

1. Right Circular Cone

Volume = (1/3)πr²h  |  Slant height l = √(r² + h²)

Curved Surface Area (CSA) = πrl  |  Total Surface Area (TSA) = πr(l + r)

2. Right Circular Cylinder

Volume = πr²h  |  CSA = 2πrh  |  TSA = 2πr(r + h)

When CSA = (1/2)TSA ⇒ 2πrh = πr(r + h) ⇒ r = h

3. Cube & Cuboid

Cube: Vol = a³  |  TSA = 6a²  |  4-Walls Area = 4a²  |  Diagonal = a√3

Cuboid: Vol = lbh  |  TSA = 2(lb + bh + hl)  |  Diagonal = √(l² + b² + h²)

N Cubes in series: LSA = 2(Na + a)a = 2(N + 1)a²

4. Sphere & Spherical Shell

Sphere: Vol = (4/3)πr³  |  Surface Area = 4πr² = πD²

Hemisphere: Vol = (2/3)πr³  |  CSA = 2πr²  |  TSA = 3πr²

Hollow Shell Mass = (4/3)π(R³ - r³) × Density
